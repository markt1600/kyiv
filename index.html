How to play

Your command post directs the war. Put Logistics Hubs on supply points (crates on gravel pads) and build generators for power. Everything is paid for gradually while it is built. Lose the command post and the war is lost.

A Design Bureau researches the ladder: FPV drones → strike drones → supersonic UCAVs, modern armour → cruise missiles, air defence, electronic warfare, and finally a Launch Complex for satellites.

Seasons matter. The calendar runs through the year. In the thaw, mud swallows anything off the roads. In winter, troops out of reach of a base (dashed circles, V) freeze and armour crawls. You can requisition city buildings as cheap, fragile forward bases.

Civilians live in the city. Ukraine receives aid while they survive; whichever side kills them loses supply income. Ruins, craters, wrecks and the dead stay where they fall.

Opening orders

  1. 2 Logistics Hubs on the nearest supply points (they light up green when you pick the icon). Supplies are your bottleneck all game.
  2. 2 Diesel Generators. Every hub and factory draws power; if the yellow bar empties, everything slows.
  3. Barracks, then queue 2 engineers so construction no longer depends on the command post, and some infantry.
  4. 2 more Logistics Hubs and another generator or two. Keep both bars from sitting at full or at zero.
  5. Design Bureau and start researching — FPV Drone Warfare or Modern Armour first.
  6. Motor Pool for tanks and artillery, then a Field Repair Depot and an anti-aircraft gun near your base.

Placements queue up: pick a building once and click as many spots as you like. The command post is already selected.
